# Privacy policy for Travel Phrases

Effective October 1, 2026.

Travel Phrases doesn't collect, store or share any personal data.

The app has no accounts, ads or analytics, and it doesn't track you. It doesn't send anything over the internet: the phrases and their audio are stored inside the app.

Your settings and saved phrases stay on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

If a phrase has no audio clip, the app asks your phone's built-in text-to-speech service to read it aloud. That service is part of your phone's operating system and follows its own privacy terms.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Questions about this policy can go to the [project's issue tracker](https://github.com/lucas-pospor/travel-phrases/issues).
