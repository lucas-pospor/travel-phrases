# Travel Phrases

A phrasebook app with 177 common phrases in 20 languages, and audio for every phrase. It works offline, and it is free and open source under the GNU GPL.

- [Download for Android](https://github.com/lucas-pospor/travel-phrases/releases/latest)
- [Source code](https://github.com/lucas-pospor/travel-phrases)
- [Privacy policy](privacy.html)

The app was built with the help of generative AI, and its voices are AI-generated.
